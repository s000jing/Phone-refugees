By placing a "red telephone booth book" among achromatic volumes symbolizing a bleak library, this webpage visualizes the covert liberation and sanctuary of students hidden within the suffocating silence. The interior background is an actual photograph of the intervention site.

The mechanism where fragments of comforting dialogue rattle down and stack like a mountain each time a token is inserted symbolizes the process in which invisible moments of solace and solidarity pile up to form a vast, "living archive."

The Easter egg, where all traces vanish like a mirage upon touching the surrounding monochrome books, exposes the overwhelming rules of the library that enforce absolute silence, while paradoxically proving the existence of "phone refugees" hidden behind that quietude in the most dramatic and poetic manner.
